What kind of friend are you? What kind of teammate?

A short, reflective questionnaire on how you connect with friends, contribute to teams and respond to everyday situations. At the end you get a personal profile and a takeaway card.

Before you start

There are no right or wrong answers. Respond based on how you generally behave, not how you think you should behave. Answer honestly, even if some of your answers seem contradictory.

This is a self-reflection activity, not a validated psychological test. Results describe tendencies you report, not fixed labels or judgements. Your answers stay on your device and are not sent anywhere.
